MODEL NOTES⌄
What this desk calculates

Each craft is a point mass propagated independently around a fixed, spherical Earth with μ = 398,600.4418 km³/s². The state is planar and inertial; the forecast uses fourth-order Runge–Kutta integration at 20 s steps and linearly interpolates closest approach between samples.

A burn is an instantaneous radial/tangential Δv at epoch. The 5 km and 1 km rings are operator triage thresholds, not collision probabilities or certified safety bounds.

Atmospheric drag, oblateness (J₂), third-body forces, attitude, finite burn duration, covariance, object size, and tracking uncertainty are omitted. The seeded incident states are synthetic, not live catalog data.
