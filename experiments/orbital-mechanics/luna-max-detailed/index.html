◎Model & assumptionsWhat this simulator includes⌄

Newtonian two-body motion. Earth is a fixed point mass with μ = 398,600.4418 km³/s². The spacecraft moves in a planar inertial frame under gravity only.

Burns are instantaneous changes to velocity. The drawn conic is the exact osculating two-body path; the flown trail uses a 5-second velocity-Verlet step.

Not modeled: atmosphere and drag, Earth oblateness, Moon/Sun gravity, thrust duration, finite spacecraft mass, or rotating-Earth launch coordinates. Earth is a spherical 6,371 km impact boundary; GEO altitude is measured from that radius.
