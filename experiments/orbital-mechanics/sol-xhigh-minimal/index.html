THE SCIENCE UNDERNEATH

A precise, simplified world.

Apogee propagates a spacecraft's position and velocity using the exact Keplerian two-body solution, solved with universal variables. Burns instantly change velocity in the orbital plane.

Earth μ 398,600.4418 km³/s²Earth radius 6,371 km

What the model includes

  • Elliptic, near-parabolic, hyperbolic, and retrograde trajectories
  • Tangential and radial impulses, with the former perpendicular to the radius and aligned with orbital motion
  • Hohmann transfers between circular, coplanar orbits
  • Surface-impact detection, including when time warp crosses the entire impact event

What it leaves out

Earth is spherical and fixed. The spacecraft has negligible mass. There is no atmosphere, drag, J₂, terrain, finite-duration thrust, fuel model, or gravity from the Moon or Sun. All motion is 2D. “GEO-class” uses the conventional approximate 35,786 km altitude; with our mean Earth radius its period is close to a sidereal day, not exact. No Earth-fixed ground track is modeled. Very large or distant orbits are educational two-body extrapolations, not mission predictions.
