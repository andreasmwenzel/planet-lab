Simple model. Real mechanics.

APOGEE is a planar orbit-raising workshop. The spacecraft follows an exact two-body Kepler solution between instantaneous velocity changes.

How to fly

  1. Choose a target altitude and load a transfer burn.
  2. Apply the burn. The far side of the orbit rises.
  3. Jump to apoapsis, then load and apply the circularization burn.

Mint is your actual orbit; amber previews the maneuver at your current position. Tangential thrust is perpendicular to the radius in your direction of orbit; radial thrust points outward. Use the zoom buttons to inspect the diagram.

Assumptions & limits

Earth is a spherical point-mass gravity source: μ = 398,600.4418 km³/s², radius = 6,371 km. No atmosphere, oblateness, Moon, Sun, spacecraft mass, fuel, or finite engine duration. Burns change velocity instantly. A trajectory intersecting Earth stops at the surface; low-altitude orbits ignore drag. Escapes have no apoapsis and are shown for a finite time horizon. This is an educational instrument, not flight software.

Orbit altitude is measured from the spherical surface. Apsis markers and preview paths describe the mathematical conic, including any subsurface segment.
