APHELION / FIELD GUIDE

Make room for wonder.

Explore a composed portrait of our solar system. Every world moves at a different pace; everything is simplified to make the system legible.

Look around
Drag with a mouse or one finger. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
Look closer
Choose a planet, then select “Inspect.” Return to the full system with the four-corner button.
Use the keyboard
Tab to the 3D view. Arrow keys rotate, + and − zoom, and Home returns to the system. Space pauses or resumes.
Take your time
Pause the motion or adjust its rate. Reset restores the original view, selection, and motion.

Planet sizes, orbital distances, and periods in the animation are deliberately altered. The reference facts describe the real worlds.
